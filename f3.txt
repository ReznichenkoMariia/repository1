Omar Khayyam (1048–1131 AD), was a Persian Muslim mathematician, astronomer, philosopher, and poet, 
whose interests also included music, mechanics, and geography. He was born and died in Nishapur, 
Iran, where he taught the philosophical theories of Ibn Sina (also known as Avicenna, 980–1037),
among other disciplines. Although Khayyam is known to later generations mainly as a poet, 
his work on algebra, mathematics, and calendar reform were of great importance.
